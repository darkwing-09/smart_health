📋 Noise Watch Setup & Verification Steps

  1. Step 1: NoiseFit → Google Fit Link

    Open NoiseFit on your phone → Settings → Connected Apps → Link with Google Fit.

  2. Step 2: Google Fit → Health Connect

    Open Google Fit → Profile → Settings gear → toggle "Sync Fit with Health Connect" to ON.

  3. Step 3: Authorize HealthAgent

    Open HealthAgent on the phone → Tap "Grant Health Connect Permissions" → Allow read access to Heart Rate, Steps, SpO₂, Sleep, and Respiratory Rate.

  4. Step 4: Enable USB Debugging (For Direct ADB Sync)

    On your phone: Settings → About Phone → Tap 'Build Number' 7 times → System → Developer Options → Enable USB Debugging. When prompted on screen, tap "Always allow from this computer".

💡 Reverse Port Forwarding Command:

To let the connected phone communicate with this workstation over USB, run:

/home/darkwing/Android/Sdk/platform-tools/adb reverse tcp:8000 tcp:8000
